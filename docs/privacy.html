BlackMail

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

BlackMail is an email app that runs on your own Windows computer. This page explains what it does with your information.

The short version

BlackMail has no servers. Your email, your sign-in and your settings stay on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your information is never sold or shared.

Signing in with Google

When you add a Gmail account, Google asks you to allow BlackMail to:

  • Read, compose, send, and permanently delete your email from Gmail — so BlackMail can show your mail, send the emails you write, and move, sort or delete emails when you ask it to (or when a sorting rule you set up says so).
  • See your email address — so BlackMail knows which account it is.

BlackMail uses this access only to give you its email features. It talks directly to Google from your computer; the developer never sees your email or your sign-in.

BlackMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored on your computer

  • Copies of your emails (so they open quickly), in BlackMail's folder on your computer.
  • Your sign-in, kept in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Your settings and sorting rules.

What BlackMail connects to

  • Your email provider (for example Google or Microsoft), to sign in, get and send your mail.
  • DuckDuckGo's icon service, to show each sender's logo. Only the sender's web address (for example "amazon.com") is sent. You can turn sender icons off in Settings.
  • Pictures inside emails, only if you choose to load them.
  • A sender's website, only when you click its Unsubscribe link.
  • GitHub, to check for and download BlackMail updates.

Removing your information

  • Remove an account in BlackMail (Settings → Accounts) to delete its sign-in.
  • Take back BlackMail's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling BlackMail removes the app. Its saved emails and settings are in the folder %APPDATA%\com.blackmail.email, which you can delete.
